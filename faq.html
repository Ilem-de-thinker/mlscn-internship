Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding internship placement, eligibility, provisional registration, and institution accreditation.

Who is eligible to apply for the MLSCN internship program?

Graduates who hold a Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Science (B.MLS) degree from an accredited university and possess a valid MLSCN provisional registration / induction certificate are eligible.

Can I apply to multiple hospitals simultaneously?

Yes, applicants can submit applications to multiple approved training institutions across different states, provided they meet each institution's specific criteria.

How do I verify if a training hospital is accredited by MLSCN?

You can use our Institution Verification tool to check the official accreditation status of any hospital or diagnostic center in Nigeria.

What documents are required during application?

Typically required documents include your B.MLS certificate or statement of result, MLSCN provisional registration, NYSC call-up letter (or exemption), valid ID, and SSCE results.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The Medical Laboratory Science Council of Nigeria (MLSCN) values your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of applicants, training institutions, and administrators using this national platform.

We collect professional certification details, academic transcripts, contact information, and institutional credentials required for internship placement and verification.

2. Data Security

All uploaded credentials and personal records are encrypted and stored securely in accordance with Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) standards.
